About our employees here at the GFT Media Foundation

Our wonderful employees here at GFT Media Foundation work and strive to make the world a better place. We appreciate our hard-working personnel and seriously couldn't do a thing without their amazing help.

Dr. Board, PhD, Historian

Dr. Board, PhD, is a historian at GFT Media specializing in historical research, archival analysis, and the interpretation of significant events and cultural developments. With a passion for making history engaging and accessible, Dr. Board contributes in-depth articles, research projects, and educational content that connect the past to contemporary audiences. His work focuses on uncovering overlooked stories, examining historical trends, and providing well-researched perspectives that inform and inspire readers. He's been a big help at testing software, as well.

Dr. Slovakov, PhD, Software Engineer

Our primary software engineer. Without Dr. Slovakov, GFT Media Foundation would be nothing. He makes high-quality software with his programming skills and never fails to let us down.
