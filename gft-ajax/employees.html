About our employees here at GFT Media

Our wonderful employees here at GFT Media Foundation work and strive to make the world a better place. We appreciate our hard-working personnel and seriously couldn't do a thing without their amazing help.

Dr. Board, PhD, Historian

Dr Board, PhD, serves here in the Foundation in many important roles, specializing in historical research, archival analysis, geography, and software testing, to name a few. Their work focuses on uncovering overlooked stories and reaching perspectives that inspire our employees. They are a large help here at the Foundation, and are always fiercely loyal. They began the creation of this website and without this decision of theirs we would not be where we are now.

Dr. Slovakov, PhD, Software Engineer

Dr Slovakov, PhD, is our primary software engineer. Without Dr. Slovakov's innovative engineering, our foundation would hardly be a thing. When away from technology, with Dr. Board they will often work together, analyzing their research and combining their intelligent minds to create new point of views, for the better of our Foundation. Dr. Slovakov is a smart, analytical yet feeling employee of ours, who will always love to help others in need, and his ideas endlessly inspires us.

Dr. Ivanov, Artist

Dr. Ivanov is the Foundation's most favored artist, and his beautiful designs make our amazing products stand out from the others. With his work, from traditionally sketched to digitally modeled, we couldn't have most of our success without him. His kindness, humor, skill, as well as loyalty much like Dr. Board, inspire us to be better to not only others but even ourselves. The three major employees Dr. Slovakov, Dr. Board, and Dr. Ivanov together as a trio create an unbreakable bond like no other.
